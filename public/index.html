Your Pinterest data

Exactly what the Studio can and cannot do.

When you connect, Pinterest shows you these permissions on its own screen. Here is what each one is used for, in plain words. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

What we use

  • user_accounts:readYour profile and analytics. To show your username and your impressions, clicks and saves.
  • boards:readYour board names. So you can pick where an approved pin goes.
  • pins:readPins made in the Studio. To show what you published.
  • pins:writeOnly pins you approve. Used after you click Approve and confirm Publish.

What we never do

  • Post without your approval. There is no auto-post setting.
  • Edit or delete your existing pins. We only add pins you approve.
  • Read your messages or followers. We never ask for that access.
  • Sell or share your data. Not to advertisers, not to anyone.
  • Keep your login. Your token is encrypted in your own browser and gone when you disconnect.

Questions
Does the Studio ever post on its own?

No. A pin is published only after you click Approve on it and then confirm Publish. If you edit an approved pin, it goes back to review.

What do I need to start?

A Pinterest business account with at least one board, and a product listing on Etsy, Shopify or your own site.

Where is my data stored?

Your Pinterest access token is encrypted inside a secure cookie in your browser. Pin drafts are saved in your own browser until you publish or clear them.

How do I disconnect and delete my data?

Click Disconnect in the Studio, or remove GrowMyPin Studio from the apps section of your Pinterest settings. You can also message us to confirm deletion.

Is this made by Pinterest?

No. GrowMyPin Studio is an independent tool that uses the official Pinterest API. Pinterest is a trademark of Pinterest, Inc.
