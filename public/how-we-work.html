What GrowMyPin does for online sellers

Most Etsy and Shopify sellers have the same problem. The products are good, the photos are good, the listings are written with care, and still the visits come in slowly. Marketplace search is crowded, paid ads get expensive, and social media asks for new content every single day. GrowMyPin exists to fix the traffic side of your business. We build a steady stream of visitors to your store using two channels that keep working long after a post is published: Pinterest and blog content.

In simple words, we do the marketing work that most sellers do not have time for. We study your shop, plan content around what your buyers search for, design pins that make people stop scrolling, write titles and descriptions that Pinterest and Google understand, and publish everything on a steady schedule. Every pin and every blog post points back to your product listings. You stay focused on making and selling your products, and we focus on bringing the right people to them.

Who we work with

We work with independent sellers and small brands who sell online and want more buyers to discover them. That includes Etsy shops, Shopify stores, sellers on eBay, and creators of digital products such as printable planners, patterns and templates. Our work fits especially well with products people love to plan, save and gift: jewelry, home decor, rugs and textiles, wall art, crochet and knitting patterns, wellness and self care products, fashion accessories and seasonal gifts.

You do not need to be a marketing expert, and you do not need an existing Pinterest following. Many of the sellers we work with start with an empty Pinterest account or one they opened years ago and never used. What matters is that you have products you are proud of and you are ready to think a little longer term. Pinterest rewards patience and consistency, and our process is built around exactly that.

1We learn your shop and your buyer

Every project starts with understanding. Before a single pin is designed, we go through your shop the way a buyer would. We look at your best products, your price range, your photos, the words you use in your listings and the reviews your customers leave. We want to know what makes your products special, who usually buys them, who they buy them for, and which moments in the year make people search for something like what you sell.

This step shapes everything that follows. A handmade silver bracelet can be a self purchase, a bridesmaid gift, a birthday present for a sister or a minimalist everyday piece, and each of those buyers searches in a different way. By mapping these buyer types early, we can create content that meets each of them at the moment they are looking. The result is marketing that feels natural to your audience instead of generic posts that try to speak to everyone at once.

2We set up a strong Pinterest foundation

Next we make sure your Pinterest presence is ready to grow. That means a proper Pinterest business account, a clear profile that tells people what your shop is about, your website or shop linked correctly, and boards that are organised the way buyers search. A board named after a real search topic helps Pinterest understand your account far better than a board with a vague or clever name.

Access is handled safely. GrowMyPin connects to Pinterest through the official Pinterest sign in, so you never share your Pinterest password with us, and you never need to share your Etsy or Shopify login either. You can see what is connected at any time and disconnect whenever you want. A clean, trusted setup at the start avoids problems later and gives your future pins the best possible chance to be shown.

3We plan content around buyer intent and the seasons

Pinterest is a planning platform. People use it to prepare for things that have not happened yet: a holiday, a wedding, a new home, a birthday, a fresh start in the new year. That is why our content plan always looks ahead. We build a calendar that mixes upcoming seasons and gift occasions with evergreen topics that people search all year long, so your account has timely content that can grow fast and steady content that keeps working in the background.

Each item in the plan has a purpose. Some pins are built to reach gift buyers, some to show your product inside a beautiful real life scene, some to answer a question or share an idea, and some to send people straight to the listing. Planning in advance also means your content is live before the busiest search weeks begin, which is when Pinterest is deciding what to show to the most people.

4We design pins and write them for search

This is where your products come to life. We create vertical pin designs made for the Pinterest feed: warm lighting, real life settings, clean layouts and short text that can be read on a small phone screen. A ring photographed on a white background becomes a ring on a hand next to fresh flowers. A rug becomes the centre of a bright, elegant room. The goal is simple: make someone stop, imagine the product in their own life and tap to learn more.

Every pin also gets a title, description and alt text written for search. We use the natural words buyers actually type, keep each pin focused on one clear idea, and avoid the keyword stuffing that makes pins look like spam. Good writing helps Pinterest understand who should see the pin, and it helps the person who sees it understand why they should click. Design and words work together, and we treat both with the same care.

5You approve everything before it goes live

Your shop is your brand, so you stay in control. Every pin we prepare goes into your review queue first. You can read the title and description, look at the design, make edits, approve the ones you like and reject anything that does not feel right. Nothing is published to your Pinterest account until you say yes. This is a core rule of how GrowMyPin works, not an optional setting.

Approving content takes only a few minutes, and it gives you a clear view of everything being said about your products. Over time you will notice that the drafts match your style more and more, because your feedback teaches us what you like. Many sellers tell us this is the part they value most: the work is done for them, but the final decision is always theirs.

6We publish steadily and add blog traffic

Once pins are approved, we publish them on a steady, natural schedule. Consistency matters more than volume on Pinterest. An account that shows up regularly with fresh, useful content earns more trust than an account that posts a large batch one day and disappears for weeks. Our publishing rhythm is designed to look and feel like a healthy, active brand account, because that is exactly what it becomes.

Alongside Pinterest, we create blog content that can rank on Google and give your pins a richer destination. Gift guides, styling ideas, care tips and how to articles give buyers a reason to read, compare and decide, with clear links to your listings throughout. Pins can point to these articles, and the articles point to your products. Together, Pinterest and blog content build two separate paths to your store that support each other.

7We measure, learn and improve every month

Publishing is only half of the job. We watch how your content performs using the analytics that Pinterest provides: how often your pins are shown, how often they are saved and, most importantly, how often people click through to your store. We look for patterns. Which product angles work best? Which visual style gets the most clicks? Which topics are rising for your niche right now?

Those answers feed straight back into the next round of planning and design. Strong ideas get more variations, weak ideas get replaced, and seasonal content is refreshed before each new season. You get clear updates about how your account is moving, written in plain language. Our aim is not to impress you with numbers that do not matter, but to keep improving the one thing that does: real visits to your store.

Why we work with patience, not shortcuts

Pinterest is not an instant results platform, and we are honest about that from day one. When an account starts publishing quality content, Pinterest first needs time to understand what the account is about and who should see it. In the early weeks, the work is building the foundation. After that, content starts to reach more people, and each new pin adds to the pins that are already working.

This is what makes Pinterest so valuable for sellers. A good pin can keep sending visitors for months, sometimes much longer, while a social media post fades within a day. We avoid shortcuts such as fake engagement, copied images or posting in bulk, because they put your account at risk and destroy the long term value we are building. Steady, original, buyer focused content is slower at the start and far stronger over time.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an existing Pinterest account?

No. We can work with a brand new Pinterest business account or improve one you already have. Many sellers start from zero.

Will you post anything without my approval?

Never. Every pin goes into your review queue first, and only the pins you approve are published to your account.

Do you need my Etsy or Shopify password?

No. We only need your public shop or product links. Pinterest is connected through the official Pinterest sign in, so you never share passwords.

Does GrowMyPin work for Shopify stores and digital products?

Yes. We work with Etsy shops, Shopify stores, eBay sellers and digital products such as patterns, printables and templates.

How soon will I see more visits?

Pinterest needs some time to learn a new account, so the first weeks are about building a strong base. After that, visits usually grow as more pins start working together. We never promise overnight results, and we share honest updates along the way.

Can I cancel?

Yes, you can cancel any time. Your published pins stay on your Pinterest account even after you cancel.
