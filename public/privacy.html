In short

  • We only access the Pinterest data needed to show your boards and analytics and to publish pins you approve.
  • Your Pinterest access token is encrypted and stored in a secure cookie in your own browser. We do not keep a copy on our servers.
  • Your drafts and settings are saved in your own browser, not on our servers.
  • Product details you enter are sent to our AI provider only to write drafts. Your Pinterest token and account data are never sent to the AI provider.
  • We never sell your data, and we never share your store identity without your separate, explicit consent.
  • You can disconnect and delete your data at any time. Steps are below.

This summary helps you read quickly. The full text below is what applies.

04How we use information

  • To connect your Pinterest account and keep you signed in.
  • To write pin drafts for the products you enter.
  • To publish pins you approve, to the board you choose.
  • To show your analytics, forecasts, strategy results and view milestones.
  • To bill paid plans and send receipts.
  • To keep the Studio secure, prevent spam and fix errors.
  • To answer your messages and complaints.

We process your data based on your consent when you connect your Pinterest account and use the Studio, and to perform our agreement with you. You can withdraw consent at any time by disconnecting.

05AI processing

To write drafts, we send the product details you entered (title, image link, niche, keywords, notes, countries, audience and chosen strategy) to our AI provider, Anthropic. The AI reads them and returns draft text.

For the free growth report, we also send the shop link you give us and the public text we can read from that shop page. The AI returns an estimate of extra views. If your Pinterest is connected, our own server adds your current views to that estimate, so your Pinterest numbers never reach the AI.

  • Your Pinterest access token, analytics and account details are never sent to the AI provider.
  • Do not put personal information about customers or other people in product notes.
  • We do not use your content to train AI models.

06Who we share data with

We share data only with the service providers needed to run the Studio, and only the data each one needs:

  • Pinterest: to connect your account, read your boards and analytics, and publish approved pins.
  • Vercel: hosts the Studio website and its server functions.
  • Anthropic: writes pin drafts and free growth reports from the product and shop details you give us. It never receives your Pinterest token or Pinterest data.
  • Razorpay: processes payments for paid plans.
  • Authorities: only when Indian law requires it.

Some of these providers process data on servers outside India. We use providers with strong security practices.

Situation

A prospective client asks us for examples of results.

What happens

We never share your store name, link or identity. We may show anonymised numbers (like a growth percentage) only if you give us separate, explicit permission first.

08How long we keep data

  • Data in your browser stays until you clear it, disconnect, or clear your browser data.
  • Account details, form answers and your Pinterest numbers snapshot stay while your account is active, and are deleted within 15 days when you ask us to delete them.
  • Hosting logs are kept for a short time to fix problems and are then deleted by our provider.
  • Payment records are kept as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires.

If we add server features in the future, such as scheduling approved pins to post on later days, we may need to store your Pinterest connection on our servers in encrypted form. We will update this policy and ask for your permission before doing so.

09Your rights and choices

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable law, you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal data we hold about you and how it is used.
  • Correct or update it.
  • Delete it.
  • Stop processing it by withdrawing your consent.
  • Hear your complaint (grievance) and respond to it.

Send requests to WhatsApp: wa.me/growmypin. We will respond within 15 days.

10How to disconnect and delete your data

  • Disconnect in the Studio: click Disconnect at the top of the dashboard. This deletes the session cookie with your access token.
  • Remove the app in Pinterest: open Pinterest settings, go to the apps section, and remove GrowMyPin Studio. This cancels the token on Pinterest's side.
  • Delete drafts and settings: use Clear done in the Studio, or clear site data for the Studio in your browser settings.
  • Ask us to confirm: message us and we will confirm deletion of anything we hold, such as payment records we are not legally required to keep.
Situation

You stop using the Studio and want everything gone.

What happens

Do all three: Disconnect, remove the app in Pinterest, and clear site data. Pins already published stay on your Pinterest account until you delete them there.

11Security

  • All traffic uses HTTPS.
  • Your access token is encrypted with AES-256-GCM before it is stored in your browser cookie.
  • Cookies holding your session are HTTP-only and secure, so page scripts cannot read them.
  • No method is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

12Children

The Studio is for businesses and is not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
